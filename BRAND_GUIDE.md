# MASH LABS Brand Guide

## Brand

**MASH LABS**

**Applied AI · Data · Engineering**

MASH LABS is an independent applied AI and data engineering lab focused on building intelligent systems around real business processes.

### Primary Brand Statement

> We engineer intelligence into business.

### Supporting Philosophy

> We build systems, not demos.

---

## What MASH LABS Does

MASH LABS designs and builds systems across:

- Data Engineering
- Analytics & Business Intelligence
- Machine Learning
- MLOps
- Applied AI
- Intelligent Automation
- Intelligent Data Systems

The objective is to connect business problems, data, intelligence, automation and engineering into practical systems that create measurable business value.

## Typography

The MASH LABS visual system uses modern sans-serif typography.

Preferred stack:

`Inter, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`

### Headings

Bold, high-contrast and concise.

### Body Copy

Clear, restrained and readable.

### Technical Labels

Uppercase with increased letter spacing.

Example:

`APPLIED AI · DATA · ENGINEERING`

---

## Voice

MASH LABS communication should be:

- Technical
- Clear
- Professional
- Business-focused
- Evidence-driven
- Practical
- Ambitious without exaggeration

Avoid presenting experimental systems as deployed commercial products unless they actually are.

---

## Visual Language

MASH LABS uses:

- Deep navy environments
- Blue-to-cyan gradients
- Technical grids
- Data-flow diagrams
- Structured system architecture
- Subtle illumination
- Minimal interfaces
- Strong typography

Avoid excessive generic AI imagery, unnecessary neon effects and visual clutter.

---

## Core Message

MASH LABS does not position AI as an isolated feature.

The lab connects:

**Data → Intelligence → Automation → Engineering → Business Value**

The goal is to build intelligent systems around real business needs.
